More Than Photographs

We preserve the moments you didn’t know you’d miss.

A wedding is rarely about the grand frame. It is your mother fixing your collar one last time. It is the cousin who cries before anyone else does. It is the silence between two people right after the pheras, when the noise finally fades and it is just the two of you.

We photograph quietly, without staging emotion, so that years later you are not looking at pictures of a wedding — you are standing inside it again.

The Experience

Quietly present. Always looking for the real moment.

I shot my first wedding in a Shahdara gali with a borrowed camera and far too much confidence. Eight years later, the gear has changed and the confidence has turned into patience — which, it turns out, is the only thing that matters.

Our team stays out of the way. We don’t interrupt rituals, we don’t choreograph tears, and we never ask you to repeat a moment that already happened. Everything you see in your album is something that truly took place.
